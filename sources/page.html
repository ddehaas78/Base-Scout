Wanted books

How it works

  • Java 26.1 and newer only, with the Trade Rebalance experiment off. Not Bedrock.
  • All novice librarians in a world share one list of trades, fixed by the seed. Each time any novice librarian gets trades, anywhere, the list moves on by one.
  • One roll = break and replace the lectern, then open the trade menu. Open it every time: the trades may only be made when the menu first opens.
  • Don't trade with the librarian before you have the right book: once you trade, his offers are locked.
  • On a server, other players' librarians move the list too. If it stops matching, find your position again.
  • In-game prices can be different (discounts, demand). The book and its level are what count.
  • Only novice (level 1) librarians. Other jobs and levels have their own lists and change nothing here. The /randomsequence command can reset the list.
